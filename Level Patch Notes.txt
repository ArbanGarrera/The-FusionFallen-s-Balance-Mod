==Level Patch Notes==
Due to the mod's various balance changes, certain levels have been rendered impossible. The following balance changes have been made to the following levels:
=====General=====

Last Stand Levels: Unbanned Melon-pult and Stallia.
                   Stallia costs sun and it seems ridiculous to target Melon-pult in particular.

Big Wave Beach: Removed low tide ambushes from normal levels.
                There is no counterplay to low tide ambushes, so they have been removed. They still appear in expansion levels.

Neon Mixtape Tour: Sun no longer falls.
                   This world takes place at night. Sun shouldn't be falling.

Zomboss Levels: The plant lineups of Zomboss levels have been changed. Zomboss levels only feature plants from their respective worlds (save for sun producers). Expansion Zomboss levels feature one Epic Quest plant in their 
                respective lineups. The Modern Day Zomboss levels feature one Gemium in their respective lineups.
