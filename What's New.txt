==What's New?==
Headbutter Lettuce's sun cost has been increased from from 175 to 450, its health has been reduced from 900 to 300 and its recharge has been reduced from 10 to 5.

Laser Bean's firing rate has been increased from 3 to 2 in order to increase its damage output and allow it to deal with the zombie waves it triggers. Its sun cost has been increased from 200 to 300 to compensate.

Snowdrop's sun cost has been increased from 250 to 275. It was still a bit too cheap for its power.

The Arma-mint Epic Quest has been moved to Pirate Seas.

Electric Peashooter now has its own Epic Quest. It was too powerful for a gemium plant. You can find the quest in Wild West where Sap-fling's Epic Quest used to be.
