==Plant Patch Notes==
Almanac: The Plants Alamanac has received numerous changes. It now provides more specific information on plants, eg. Firing rates, Stun duration, Cooldowns. Various plants have had their entries changed based on the various
balancing changes they have received. 

Plant availability: An issue with the later worlds of Plants VS. Zombies 2 is that the plants stop being catered to the zombies and mechanics of the worlds. This is especially prevalent in Neon Mixtape Tour and Modern Day. None of
the plants obtained in these worlds counter any of the worlds' zombies and mechanics. The player often resorts to using instant use plants to get rid of the zombies. Worlds after Far Future also give you less plants, usually five
compared to the seven of the earlier worlds. The goal is for each world to have 7-8 plants. The following plants have been moved:

Snow Pea: Now obtained from Ancient Egypt Level 24. It's better to have this plant early than later. Snow Pea isn't that effective in Kongfu Temple because of the weapon stands and Hammer Zombie.
Twin Sunflower: Now obtained from Ancient Egypt Level 21.
Squash: Now obtainable from the store after completing Ancient Egypt Level 6. A plant as simple as Squash shouldn't be locked behind several difficult levels.
Grapeshot: Now obtainable from the store after completing Ancient Egypt Level 24-1. Grapes were widely cultivated in Ancient Egypt to make wine.
Sling Pea: Now obtainable from the store after completing Ancient Egypt Level 28. Fits in a lobber-centric world.
Garlic: Now obtained from Pirate Seas Level 18. Replaces Spikerock. The planks make it easy for it to manipulate zombies.
Pea-nut: Now obtainable from the store after completing Pirate Seas Level 10. The player is used to Peashooters and Wall-nuts by this point in the game, so why not give them a combination of the two?
Marigold: Now obtainable from the store after completing Pirate Seas Level 24-1. A plant that allows you to farm money should be locked behind a really hard level and the money plant fits with pirates wanting treasure.
Iceweed: Now obtainable from the store after completing Pirate Seas Level 29. This plant should not be obtainable before you get normal Spikeweed and it fits in with the same world you get the original Spikeweed.
Torchwood: Now obtained from Wild West Level 15. At this point you have enough Peashooter options to use with this plant.
Cactus: Now obtainable from the store after completing Wild West Level 10. Fits with the desert theme.
Cran Jelly: Now obtainable from the store after completing Wild West Level 24-1. It fits in the world since 19th century America was where the cranberry was first cultivated.
Chilly Pepper: Now obtainable from the store as a ticket plant. It's still pretty strong that it should be locked behind tickets.
Power Lily: Now obtained from Far Future Level 21. Fits in a plant food-centric world.
Imitater: Now obtainable from the store after completing Far Future Level 10. It turns into a clone of a plant and cloning is a futuristic concept.
Moon Bean: Now obtainable from the store after completing Far Future Level 24. It revolves around a Far Future mechanic so it belongs in this world.
Hypno-shroom: Now obtained from Dark Ages Level 8. It is more useful in Dark Ages than other worlds due to the larger supply of high toughness zombies like Knight Zombies and zombies under the effects of potions.
Scaredy-shroom: Now obtained from Dark Ages Level 11. This plant should be obtained earlier. By the time you get it, you've already beaten the main levels.
Pumpkin: Now obtained from Dark Ages Level 18. Its Halloween aesthetic fits with night levels and it has synergy with Scaredy-shroom.
Plantern: Now obtainable from the store after completing Dark Ages Level 18-1. Completely useless without plant food. It shouldn't be locked behind extremely hard levels. Being a store plant is more reasonable.
Witch Hazel: Now obtainable from the store after completing Dark Ages Level 24. Not that strong so it should be a gemium instead of a ticket plant.
Bowling Bulb: Now obtained from Big Wave Beach Level 8.
Sea-shroom: Now obtained from Big Wave Beach Level 11. This plant should be obtained earlier. By the time you get it, you've already beaten the main levels.
Chomper: Now obtained from Big Wave Beach Level 14. Chomper can serve as a counter to Surfer Zombie, as it kills the zombie without causing it to drop his board.
Ghost Pepper: Now obtained from Big Wave Beach Level 21. Ghost Pepper can be planted over the water and helps dealing with Octo Zombies and Fisherman Zombies in the back.
Lava Guava: Now obtained from Frostbite Caves Level 14. It can warm plants and can be positioned near slider tiles so zombies walk right into his lava pool.
Jalapeno: Now obtained from Frostbite Caves Level 17. Jalapeno can serve as a counter to Troglobite, as it covers the zombie's range and it can kill the Yeti Imps without needing to break the ice blocks first.
Pyre Vine: Now obtained from Frostbite Caves Level 23. It can serve as a fast warmer and the player isn't railroaded into picking only fire plants since it doesn't take up space on the lawn.
Starfruit: Now obtained from Frostbite Caves Level 26. Replaces Rotobaga. Had a similar role to Rotobaga and it can take advantage of the slider tiles.
Umbrella Leaf: Now obtained from Lost City Level 14. Umbrella Leaf can serve as a counter to Lost Pilot Zombie and Lost Raider Zombie.
Toadstool: Now obtained from Lost City Level 22. Its a toad in a world full of bug enemies. What more can I say?
Escape Root: Now obtained from Lost City Level 29. It is a plant that functions like a trap so it fits with a world full of trap tiles.
Turkey-pult: Now obtainable from the store as a gemium plant after completing Lost City Level 31. It isn't strong enough to be a ticket plant. 
Sweet Potato: Now obtained from Neon Mixtape Tour Level 9. Replaces Thyme Warp. It's too simple of a plant to be in an epic quest and it has synergy with Phat Beet.
Spikerock: Now obtained from Neon Mixtape Tour Level 11. It fits thematically with the world as a plant with 'rock' in its name would fit with the rock 'n' roll world.
Hurrikale: Now obtained from Neon Mixtape Tour Level 17. Replaces Garlic. Hurrikale can serve as a counter to Breakdancer Zombie by pushing back the zombies it kicks.
Shrinking Violet: Now obtained from Neon Mixtape Tour Level 21. Replaces Spore-shroom. All of the zombies have dangerous special abilities and Shrinking Violet can turn them off or weaken them.
Spore-shroom: Now obtained from Neon Mixtape Tour Level 29. It isn't that essential and doesn't interact with any of the zombies or mechanics of the world.
Caulipower: Now obtainable from the store after completing Neon Mixtape Tour 39. This is the level that introduces Caulipower, there's no reason why it shouldn't be available after its completion.
Homing Thistle: Now obtained from Jurassic Marsh Level 11. Homing Thistle can attack the zombies moved to the back of the lawn by pterodactyls. Splitpea can't deal with the Bucketheads on its own.
Sap-fling: Now obtained from Jurassic Marsh Level 17. Fits thematically with the world as tree sap can form amber. There are many instances of amber containing fossils. Also serves to mitigate the T-Rex's effects.
Blooming Heart: Now obtained from Jurassic Marsh Level 20. It can serve as a counter to Jurassic Bully.
Primal Sunflower: Now obtained from Jurassic Marsh Level 29.
Snap Pea: Now obtainable from the store after completing Jurassic Marsh Level 3. Replaces Grapeshot. It has a dinosaur-like appearance.
Murkadamia Nut: Now obtained from Modern Day Level 4. It has gameplay applications as a counter to Newspaper Zombie.
Nightshade: Now obtained from Modern Day Level 14.
Gloom Vine: Now obtained from Modern Day Level 20. It doesn't make sense to have this in the store at the start of the game when the player doesn't have any shadow plants yet.
Thyme Warp: Now obtained from Modern Day Level 27. Fits thematically with the world.
Angel Starfruit: Now obtainable from the store after completing Modern Day Level 13. A plant this powerful should be saved for the final world.
Snowdrop: Now obtained from KongFu Temple Level 6. Replaces Snow Pea. A better fit for this world as it can ignore weapon stands and Hammer Zombie.
Explode-O-Nut: Now obtainable from the store after completing KongFu Temple Level 22. Replaces Power Lily. Fits in an explosive-themed world.
Acesparagus: Now obtained from Sky City Level 3. Doesn't deserve to be a gemium.
Rotobaga: Now obtained from Sky City Level 8. Replaces Moon Bean. It is a a flying plant and it is obtained there in the Chinese version.
Loquat: Now obtained from Sky City Level 17. It should be available earlier.

Plant selections in certain levels have been adjusted to fit with the changes to plant availability.

Squash, Torchwood, Pumpkin, Plantern, Ghost Pepper, Umbrella Leaf, Sweet Potato, Sap-fling, Blooming Heart, Murkadamia Nut and Gloom Vine's Epic Quest levels are still available to play, but you will be rewarded with money instead of the plant 
upon their completion.

Slow recharging plants: All plants with a slow recharge have had their recharge adjusted to 40 seconds. This is to prevent spamability of certain plants.

Chilling attacking plants: Their chill now lasts for only slightly longer than their firing rates to prevent them from affecting zombies even after they die. Now the plants will need to be kept alive to reapply their chill, 
encouraging players to take better care of their plants. This also applies to attacking plants that apply other types of status effects like Sap-fling, Goo Peashooter and Dark Matter Dragonfruit.

Families: Certain plants have had their families changed for better plant distribution and certain family placements being questionable.

Mints: Certain mints now boost certain plants outside of their families. Certain plants have aspects that fit in multiple families.
